Metabolism can be interpreted as a resource allocation problem: cells must allocate limited enzyme, energy, carbon, nitrogen, and redox resources among competing biochemical tasks.

This note will eventually collect the basic dictionary between metabolic theory and consumer choice theory: budgets, preferences, shadow prices, substitution effects, and growth laws.
